# Brag Plan: YardSight AI (GodownOS)

## What is this app?
An autonomous, edge-optimized industrial vision and multimodal logistics intelligence operating system that transforms legacy Dahua and ONVIF CCTV cameras into a unified logistics command center with sub-200ms streaming, homography ANPR, and specular hydrocarbon leak detection.

## The angle
Commercial freight terminals and godowns handle thousands of trucks daily while drowning in blind camera angles, acute-angle license plate distortion, and undetected hazardous spills. YardSight bridges 32 legacy NVR channels with real-time edge AI without expensive server-side GPU clusters or cloud streaming bandwidth costs.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-impact cyber-industrial title sequence with pulsing tactical HUD grid and an acute 45-degree angle truck entry at the gate: "32 BLIND CCTV CHANNELS. ZERO EXTRA GPUS. ONE GODOWN OS."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **32-Channel Real-Time Dahua NVR Matrix**: Sub-10ms latency live P2P optical hardware streaming across 4/8/9/16/32-split layouts.
- **OpenCV 4.10 Perspective Homography ANPR**: High-precision 4-corner perspective unwarping rectifying acute 45° distorted plates into orthogonal readable text strips for both US Commercial Carriers and Indian HSRP formats.
- **Concrete Specular Hydrocarbon Leak Detection**: Real-time thin-film interference modeling detecting diesel and hydraulic fluid spills ($n \approx 1.47$) vs benign rainwater ($n = 1.33$), with automatic OSHA 1910.120 absorbent boom calculation.
- **Nimbus Multimodal VLM Copilot**: Natural language query over live CCTV feeds and yard telemetry with automated WhatsApp & Twilio dispatch.

## Outro / punchline
1-click cryptographically sealed PDF audit report generation + WhatsApp incident dispatch: "YARDSIGHT AI. AUTONOMOUS INDUSTRIAL LOGISTICS VISION."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Gate Inbound**: Heavy freight carrier arrives at South Gate at a severe 45° angle.
2. **Homography Unwarp**: OpenCV homography normalizes the plate into a crisp orthogonal strip and extracts license + USDOT/e-Way bill credentials.
3. **Hazard Detection**: Forklift hydraulic leak on loading bay concrete triggers specular chromatic alert with boom deployment sizing.
4. **Instant Dispatch**: Single-click WhatsApp emergency notification dispatched with camera coordinates and snapshot link.
